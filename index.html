About Moduliv

Moduliv was created to serve people who need homes.

We have engineered a high-quality, efficient building system, established a supply chain, and formalised an on-site construction process that builds homes faster for less cost.

  • Steel-framed building system
  • NHBC certified
  • Homes England grant approved
  • Main mortgage lender pre-approved

By simplifying the existing process, we honour the principle that everyone should have a high-quality home to live in and enjoy.

Meet the Team

The people building the future of housing.

Decades of experience across UK real estate, capital markets, fund management and Islamic finance.

Saadat Khan

Founder & Chairman

His mission is to end global homelessness and solve the UK housing crisis.

He has over 20+ years’ experience in the UK real estate sector, including strategy, finance, land sourcing, planning, investment, development, fund structuring, advisory and product development.

Saadat has developed several market-leading initiatives such as the world’s first asset-backed Sukuk for £200m and the first modular house for Manchester City Council.

Mark Fahy

Senior Advisor

14 years at the London Stock Exchange as Head of UK Small & Mid-Cap Companies.

Mark initially worked as an Institutional Fund Manager running a UK Equities portfolio, and supported a vast range of companies on the Road to IPO on both AIM and the Main Market.

He is working with the team on the financial strategy to drive growth towards an IPO in January 2029, and also works at the British Business Bank supporting SMEs.

Robert Leon
